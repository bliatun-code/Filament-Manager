## Printer images

Official printer thumbnails from Bambu Studio (Bambu Lab) and PrusaSlicer
(Prusa Research) are redistributed under AGPL-3.0. The original files are
included unmodified. Per-file source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
`ui/src/assets/printers/sources.json`; upstream license texts are beside them.
Separate AI-processed transparent illustrations based on eight of those
thumbnails are used for display. Their sources, processing prompts and output
hashes are recorded in `ui/src/assets/printers/companion-derivatives.json`.
The original licensed files remain included alongside the derivatives.
Some closely related printer revisions share a family image. Models without
suitable licensed artwork use the application's neutral illustration.
These images identify supported equipment and do not imply endorsement.
