# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Filament Manager welcomes constructive participation from people of every
background and experience level. Be respectful, assume good intent while
asking for clarification, and keep discussion focused on the project and its
users.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Giving specific, actionable, and considerate feedback.
- Respecting different experience levels, languages, identities, and points of
  view.
- Protecting other people's privacy and removing sensitive data from reports,
  logs, screenshots, and examples.
- Accepting moderation decisions and correcting mistakes without retaliation.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, publishing private information, and sustained disruption
are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue and pull-request
discussion, review, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting And Enforcement

For an ordinary concern, contact the repository maintainers through GitHub with
the minimum public detail needed to request follow-up. Do not publish personal
or sensitive evidence in an issue. For platform abuse or an immediate safety
concern, use GitHub's private content-reporting and blocking tools.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, code, issues,
and other material that conflict with this standard, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Reports will be considered in context and
handled as privately as the available GitHub channels allow.
